  Hello! I’m a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Toronto Scarborough.
  I work with [Dr. Philip Monahan](https://phijomo.github.io/) and [Dr. Yoonjung Kang](https://www.yoonjungkang.com/), investigating how the human brain transforms speech sounds into meaning.
  My research combines behavioral methods and EEG to study speech perception, phonological representation, and cue weighting across languages.
---

Before coming to Toronto, I completed my PhD in linguistics and cognitive science at the University of Delaware under the supervision of [Dr. Arild Hestvik](https://udel.edu/~hestvik/). I also received training in applied linguistics as a master student at Beijing Foreign Studies University.

## Current research questions

- How do language-specific phonological patterns shape phonetic perception? [SNL 2025 poster on phonological patterns]({{ '/assets/files/flap_snl.pdf' | relative_url }})
- How does the brain weight temporal and spectral cues to distinguish speech sounds? [SNL 2025 poster on temporal and spectral cues]({{ '/assets/files/laryngeal_snl.pdf' | relative_url }})
- How is prototypicality information about speech sounds encoded in the brain?
